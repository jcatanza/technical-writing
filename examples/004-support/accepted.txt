"Support" means extra evidence that sits beside the decision and does not make it. The decision comes from one measure only: the average score at a limit of 0.5.

The support has two parts.

**Does the second chart agree?**

- The second chart is another score. It averages over the 20 zones.
- Unlike the average score, it needs no limit. It does not depend on the 0.5 setting.
- If both charts point at the same values, that adds confidence. If they differ, I say so in the report and keep the choice that the rule made.

**The size split**

- Nights differ. Some nights have one to three events, and some have dozens.
- A low smoothing level can delete a real event on a busy night.
- I compute the score for each smoothing level in each size group.
